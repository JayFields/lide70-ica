The CanoScan LiDE 70 driver is installed.

If Image Capture or Preview is open, quit it and open it again. Then connect the scanner and choose it in Image Capture, or in Preview with File > Import from Scanner.

There is nothing to calibrate: every scan calibrates itself from the scanner's internal white strip.

To uninstall, run in Terminal:
sudo rm -rf "/Library/Image Capture/Devices/LiDE70Scanner.app"
sudo pkgutil --forget local.lide70ica.module
